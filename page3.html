01 / Our Origin Story

Three friends. One team. A Charleston tradition.

In our fictional version of the Charleston RiverDogs’ beginnings, Sarah, Megan, and Eric shared a dream: to create a baseball team that would bring Charleston together. While tossing a baseball around one afternoon, they imagined a ballpark filled with cheering families, proud local fans, and summer nights that people would remember long after the final out. They decided to turn that idea into the Charleston RiverDogs, a team built around exciting baseball and a strong connection to its community.

Each founder brought a different skill to getting the team off the ground. Sarah, the Data Analyst, studied potential attendance, ticket prices, and costs to help shape a realistic business plan. Megan, in the IT Department, built the team’s digital presence so fans could discover the RiverDogs, find game information, and explore ticket and merchandise options. Eric, in Business Operations, organized budgets, coordinated staffing, and developed marketing and funding plans to help bring their first season to life.

Together, they built more than a place to watch baseball: they created a business centered on the fans. Today, in our project, Sarah, Megan, and Eric work together on ticket sales, merchandise, technology, financial planning, and game-day operations. From promoting the next home game to improving concession ordering, their mission remains the same: give Charleston a team to cheer for and an experience worth coming back for.

Our Game Plan

A winning team, on and off the field.

  1. Connect with fans.

  2. Run the business.

02 / Meet the Founders

Three roles. One winning experience.

Our student founders bring different skills to the same mission: supporting the RiverDogs business and creating an enjoyable experience for every fan.

Sarah

Data Analyst

Sarah turns business data into better decisions. She analyzes ticket sales, attendance, merchandise trends, marketing results, and financial performance to help the team forecast demand, evaluate opportunities, and understand what fans enjoy.

Megan

IT Department

Megan manages the technology that connects fans with the team. She maintains the website and app, supports online ticketing and merchandise systems, and troubleshoots technical issues. She also helps develop digital improvements, including the proposed concession portal.

Eric

Business Operations

Eric oversees the business side of the ballpark. He coordinates daily operations, staffing, budgets, inventory, and marketing plans while contributing to financial planning, funding, and investment decisions that support the organization’s goals.

04 / The Fan FAQ

A few questions from the stands.

What does your team do behind the scenes?

Our project covers the business of baseball: ticket sales, marketing, merchandise, technology, daily operations, and financial planning. Sarah handles data analysis, Megan manages IT, and Eric coordinates business operations.

Can we plan a group night at the ballpark?

In our fictional business, absolutely! Families, student organizations, and local businesses could contact our office to discuss group seating and a night cheering together. Matching outfits are optional; enthusiastic cheering is encouraged.

Where would I find tickets and team merchandise?

Our project envisions a team website and app where fans can explore ticket options and shop for RiverDogs gear, along with in-person assistance during posted hours. This About Us page is a demonstration and does not process purchases.

Are you working on any game-day improvements?

Yes! One proposal is a QR-code concession portal where fans could order food, track its progress, and receive a pickup notification. In-person ordering would remain available. This supports our wider goal of improving the entire ballpark experience.

Do I have to know every baseball rule to have fun?

Only if you are volunteering to umpire! Our fictional business welcomes longtime fans and first-time visitors alike. Come for the baseball, the company, or an excuse to wear your favorite team gear.

Is this an official Charleston RiverDogs website?

This is a fictional student business project inspired by the Charleston RiverDogs. The founding story, staff roles, contact information, and hours were created for the assignment. This page does not accept real purchases or bookings.
